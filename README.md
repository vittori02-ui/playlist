# **My song**

### Explanation and introduction
This app is used to manage your important audio files or downloaded songs, where the main screen is the place where you find all your
audio files together that you put in the MyPlaylist folder created by the app inside your Android phone's Music folder. 
From the main screen, you can create playlists to categorize the audio files, listen to them with a tool to go forward, backward, pause,
use the slider and the widget, and it also works with the screen off. If you close the app, the audio stops along with the whole app, of course. 
In the app, you can see a progressive number indicating how many files there are, the song image (otherwise a default one is used), the title, 
and the artist. The audio system lowers the volume if a call or another app that generates audio comes in, to give space to the call or whatever. 
There's also a search bar that searches in real time and checks title or artist, along with a system that shows the album cover with the image of 
the song and the slider that moves and the time that has passed

### Inspiration
It comes from my last trip to Greece when I forgot to download the songs for when I was on the plane, and the ones I had were handled badly by
the software on my phone

### Download instructions:  
It's important that your Android version is 10 or higher because some libraries only work from version 10 onwards.  
To add audio files, the app will create a folder called 'MyPlaylist' inside the Android Music folder. Look for it in your phone's 
file manager and move your audio files there so you'll see them on the app's main page and can sort them into your playlists.  
P.S. If the folder doesn't appear right away, allow permissions for media and audio data, then close and reopen the app 
and check again if the folder is there.
